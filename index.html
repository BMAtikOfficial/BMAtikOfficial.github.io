Thesis research Leading University · EEE

IEC 61724-1-Aligned Industrial Solar Monitoring and DNN Forecasting System

A site-specific solar PV monitoring and forecasting project designed around IEC 61724-1-aligned measurement concepts. The planned system uses ESP32-S3-based data logging at one-minute resolution and DNN forecasting for 15-, 30- and 60-minute horizons.

  • Planned 100-day monitoring campaign with approximately 144,000 raw one-minute records.
  • Solar-active data prepared for chronological model training, validation and testing.
  • Focus on practical campus PV monitoring, data quality and short-horizon forecasting.

Supervisor: Md. Niaz Morshedul Haque · Co-supervisor: Thonmoy Chowdhury Suhel

Power systems + AI Research project

LLM-Based Power-System Operator Copilot

A staged research pipeline for an LLM-based operator copilot evaluated on the IEEE 39-bus benchmark, combining power-system validation with grounded language-model assistance.

  • CIM-aligned state representation and retrieval-assisted operator support.
  • AC power flow, OPF and N-1 validation workflows.
  • Fail-safe abstention, grounding, auditability, robustness and replay-oriented evaluation.
